Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

This policy explains how Faith Established ("we") handles information on this website and in the tools we use to make our videos.

This website

This website does not ask for, collect or store any personal information. It has no sign-up, no comments, no analytics and no advertising cookies. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts; those services may record standard technical information, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Faith Established Agent

"Faith Established Agent" is an internal tool used only by the Faith Established team. It signs in to the channel's own Google account to read our prayer schedule from Google Sheets and to save the videos we produce to our own Google Drive. It does not access any other person's Google account or data, and the information it handles is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

YouTube

When you watch our videos on YouTube, your use of YouTube is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and YouTube's Terms of Service.

Changes

If we add features that collect information, such as a prayer wall, we will update this policy before they go live.
